What to look for

A straight line can separate two clusters, but usually cannot describe moons or circles. Local methods and trees can learn more complex shapes. A more detailed boundary does not always generalize better: compare training and test accuracy as you increase noise or depth.

30% of each class is held out for testing; models train only on the remaining 70%. The same seed reproduces the data and split. This is an educational demonstration with synthetic data, one split, and boundaries approximated on a grid. Logistic regression uses 900 gradient-descent steps; the tree uses Gini splits and at least two observations per leaf.
